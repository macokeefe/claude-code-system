01

What changed — and why the model is no longer the moat

Supply side · build cost fell off a cliff

In one year: SAM 3 (Meta, Nov 2025) — text-promptable "segment anything by noun phrase," no per-class training; DINOv3 (Aug 2025) — a frozen backbone at SOTA "often without fine-tuning," incl. a satellite variant; self-hostable VLMs; and edge boards that halved in price (Jetson Orin Nano Super, $249; Hailo-10H, ~$130 / 2.5W).

Labeling campaigns and per-call API fees — the two historical build-cost sinks — are gone.

Demand side · regulation created dated buyers

Hard deadlines now compel spend: EPA LCRI lead-line inventory due Nov 2027; CMS never-event non-payment for hospital-acquired pressure injuries; the European Accessibility Act (live, penalties to 4% of revenue); and — the standout — workers-comp insurers moving from discount to mandate: Zurich now conditions coverage on jobsite cameras after a pilot cut claims >70%.

Caution the verification kept surfacing: separate the mandate from the money — the methane fee slipped to 2034 even as monitoring mandates held.

The durable question is not "can vision do this?" — it now can. It is: who has a budgeted, non-discretionary reason to pay, and is that reason already in force? Crowd into the compliance / data layer, not the perception layer.

The recommendation

Build this firstWeighted 6.83 · 0 claims struck

PoleLedger — stampable pole-condition records for wildfire liability

Highest weighted composite and highest impact in the set, and the only top-tier concept whose every load-bearing claim survived adversarial verification. It attaches to the most solvent, most structurally compelled buyer in the study: electric utilities facing existential wildfire liability (PG&E: ~$30B, Chapter 11), spending from regulator-mandated wildfire-mitigation budgets against a 180M-pole distribution blind spot that drives most utility ignitions.

Beats #2 ErgoClaim on

Impact magnitude (catastrophic-fire prevention vs. incremental injury reduction) and budget-pool size (~$100B utility wildfire investment need).

Beats #3 LineProof on

Durability — LineProof is a largely one-time inventory buy against a shrinking unknown count; PoleLedger compounds into a longitudinal condition-history moat + a hard-dollar pole-rent-audit upsell.

Eyes open, not a slam dunk: a funded incumbent, Noteworthy AI, already ships the near-identical fleet-mounted drive-by product incl. leaning-pole detection (verified, High confidence). This is a race on workflow and data lock-in — so the first 90 days spend zero time out-detecting them and all of it on the PE-stamped compliance artifact and the joint-use attachment-audit revenue hook.
04

The graveyard — eight ways CV companies die

The whole CV market is only ~$20–24B of revenue, yet AV alone drew ~$21B of investment. Capital over-funded the perception layer; revenue lived in deployment and compliance. The recommendations dodge patterns 3–8 cleanly.

05

How this report was produced

  1. Research (16 parallel agents). 3 capability analysts (models / edge / sensing), 10 domain scouts, and market-funding / graveyard / timing analysts — all live web search, structured & cited.
  2. Synthesis memo. Findings compressed to one page plus a banned "crowded categories" list, to force contrarian ideation.
  3. Ideation (3 lenses × 7). Contrarian, capability-first, and pain-first ideators generated 21 raw concepts.
  4. Cut. Dedup + anti-goal kill → 10 survivors.
  5. Scoring (3 judges). Each survivor scored independently on the weighted rubric; scores averaged; top 8 advanced.
  6. Deep-dive → verify (pipeline). Full cited one-pagers, then 3 adversarial refuters per load-bearing claim (2-of-3 = struck).
  7. Synthesize + critique. Lead synthesist wrote the ranked report; a completeness critic checked 10 quality gates; one revision pass fixed the flagged deficiencies.
