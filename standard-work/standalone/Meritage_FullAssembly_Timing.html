Seat frame = last required feeder (gates the start) Full assembly (runs straight through — kit is fully ready) Kit-ready line
Because Seat frame (47) finishes after Arms (32), Back frame (43.5) and Trellis (22.5), the whole kit is ready at t = 47 — so Full Assembly never starves mid-build. The real gap is before 47: the 2 Full-Assembly people have nothing to assemble yet.

What the 2 Full-Assembly people do each ~47-min cycle

Switch to packing the moment they hand off an assembled unit. The next kit won't be ready for ~18 min (Seat frame is still building), and packing takes ~18 min — so it fills the gap almost perfectly: assemble → pack → assemble, triggered by the Seat-frame "done" signal.
Bottleneck = Seat frame at 47 min → ~9 units/day, just over the 42-min takt. Packing already absorbs the FA pair's spare time, so to get under takt the cleaner move is a third hand on Seat frame (not more help at assembly).
