Meritage 3-Seater — Assembly Line Floor Plan

As-built layout · 8 operators · times in minutes per unit

Flow: the cart pre-stages parts to all feeder benches. Feeders (Connectors, Arms, Back frame, Trellis, Seat frame) run in parallel, then converge into Full Assembly, which hands the finished sofa to Packing & Ship. Seat frame (47 min, rust) is the bottleneck — it sets the line pace at ~9/day. Packing has no dedicated operators; the 2 Full-Assembly operators pack during their wait time.
