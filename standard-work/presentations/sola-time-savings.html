Already handled · just make them the standard

The two biggest steps aren't our constraint

Seat Support Frame Assembly 2-person standard

Most labor in the sofa — but the team already puts two people on it. The slats are independent, so it splits cleanly.

Rule: on Sola-heavy days, always two.

Connector Pre-Assembly kept ahead

The connector lady builds to a buffer and tops up wherever stock runs low — so the line pulls finished connectors off the shelf and never waits.

It costs labor, but it isn't on the line's clock — so it shouldn't count as a bottleneck.

Rule: keep the connector buffer ahead of the line.

Set those two as standard and move on — the time to chase is elsewhere.

The opportunity · one of these is free

Frame work: overlap it, then fixture it build time

Frame Sub-Assembly is two separate pieces built back-to-back — 4-leg (8:30) then 5-leg (10:30). Build them at the same time and 19:00 becomes 10:30.

Then a frame-alignment fixture trims the 38 min of frame work further — less hand-clamping, less re-checking, fewer misalignments.

Build time: ~75 → ~66 free, → ~60 with a fixture.
