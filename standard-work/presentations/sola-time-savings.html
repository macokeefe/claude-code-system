The bottleneck

One station sets the whole line's pace

Opportunity 1 · biggest win by far

Break up Seat Support Frame Assembly bottleneck labor

It's 51 min of one person screwing slats one after another — and it's both the line bottleneck and a third of all labor.

  • Put two people on it — the slats are independent, so the work halves to ~25 min with no new tools.
  • Or speed the task — a multi-head / pneumatic driver and a slat-holding fixture cut the repetitive screwing.

Opportunity 2 · helps every config

Fix the connector workflow build time labor

Connector Pre-Assembly (13:20) + Connector Plate Installation (14:52) = ~28 min, and both sit on the critical path for a single build.

  • Connector Plate — we drill four holes in every plate on the line, then blow out debris. Pre-drilled plates delete that work.
  • Connector Pre-Assembly — 50 seconds × ~16 connectors, hand-prepped one at a time. Batch it or have connectors arrive pre-assembled.

These are shared steps — the same work runs on all three Sola configs. Fix once, every Sola gets faster. Target: 8–14 min/sofa.

Opportunity 3 · free

Build the two frame sub-pieces in parallel build time

Frame Sub-Assembly is literally two separate pieces built one after the other — the 4-leg section (8:30) then the 5-leg section (10:30) = 19:00 by one person.

Today — one person, back to back:

4-leg · 8:30→5-leg · 10:30 = 19:00

Two people, at the same time:

4-leg · 8:30
5-leg · 10:30 = 10:30

Same labor, no new tools — just overlap it. ~8.5 min off the build time, free. A frame-alignment fixture would trim the 38 min of frame work further.
