Opportunity 1 · biggest quick win

Build the two frame sub-pieces in parallel flow

Frame Sub-Assembly is two independent pieces built one after the other: the 4-leg section (8:30) then the 5-leg section (10:30) = 19:00 by one person.

Today — one person, serial:

4-leg · 8:30→5-leg · 10:30 = 19:00

Two people, in parallel:

4-leg · 8:30 
5-leg · 10:30 = 10:30 wall-clock

Same labor, no new tools — just overlap the work. Saves ~8.5 min of build time per unit, straight off the critical path.

Opportunity 2 · highest leverage

Fix the connector workflow labor flow

Connector Pre-Assembly (13:20) + Connector Plate Installation (14:52) = ~28 min of every Sola, and both sit on the critical path.

  • Connector Pre-Assembly — 16 connectors hand-prepped one at a time. The SWI itself flags this "inefficient" (and "unnecessary" on the no-arm configs). Batch it, fixture it, or have connectors arrive pre-assembled.
  • Connector Plate Installation — drilling 4 holes in every plate on the line, then clearing debris. Pre-drilled plates remove the drilling entirely.

These are shared steps — the same work appears on all three Sola configs. Fix it once, every Sola gets faster. Realistic target: 8–14 min/unit.

Sola Lounge — Time Savings6 / 10
Opportunity 3

Fixture the frame assemblies labor flow

Frame Sub-Assembly + Frame Assembly = 38 min combined, both on the critical path. Today they're aligned by hand with bar clamps — "hold extrusions flush and aligned," "verify no extrusions are misaligned."

  • A dedicated assembly fixture / jig holds the extrusions square automatically — less clamping, less re-checking, less re-work.
  • Removes the most variable, operator-dependent part of the build (alignment), which also improves consistency.

Estimated 3–6 min/unit, plus fewer defects from misalignment.

Sola Lounge — Time Savings7 / 10
Opportunity 4 · do this first

Measure the two blind steps visibility

Seat Support Frame Assembly and Frame Prep have no recorded time. They're real work that happens on every unit, but they're invisible in every number on these slides.

  • We can't improve — or even correctly staff — what we haven't measured.
  • A quick time-study on both closes the gap and may surface a hidden chunk of time.
  • Cheapest action on the list: a stopwatch and one build.

Every other estimate here is conservative because these two are still zero.
