Already handled · make it the standard

Seat Support Frame Assembly: always staff it with two standard

It's the most labor in the sofa, but it has never been our bottleneck — because the team already puts two people on it. The slats are independent, so the work splits cleanly.

The only risk is not doing it on a busy day. So let's make it the rule, not a habit:

On Sola-heavy days, Seat Support Frame Assembly is always a two-person station.

Opportunity 1 · helps every config

Fix the connector workflow build time labor

  • Pre-drilled plates. Today we drill four holes in every plate on the line, then blow out the debris. Order plates pre-drilled and that work disappears.
  • Pre-kit / batch the connectors. 50 seconds × ~16 connectors, hand-prepped one at a time. Batch them up front, or have them arrive assembled.

Target: 8–14 min off every Sola.

Opportunity 2 · one of them is free

Frame work: overlap it, then fixture it build time

Frame Sub-Assembly is two separate pieces built back-to-back — 4-leg (8:30) then 5-leg (10:30). Build them at the same time and 19:00 becomes 10:30.

Then a frame-alignment fixture trims the 38 min of frame work further — less hand-clamping, less re-checking, fewer misalignments.
