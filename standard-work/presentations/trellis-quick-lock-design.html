Quick-Lock Trellis — Seat Support Concept

Same bolted-on, fully removable trellis — re-engineered so each slat drops in and locks with one turn instead of four screws · concept sketch, not to scale

Before  →  After

TODAY · screwed slat-by-slat
~4 screws driven on every slat × every slat = the bulk of the 32:30 step. Detachable, but slow to build and slow to service.
PROPOSED · keyhole rails + 1 quarter-turn lock
Stud drops through the keyhole, slides to seat, one quarter-turn locks it. No welding — every slat still comes out individually for service.

How the joint works

1 · Keyhole slot + stud
2 · Quarter-turn cam lock
3 · Swap a broken slat
32:30 → ~10–12 min

~20 minutes saved per sofa (≈16% of total Sola labor), on all three configs — by replacing ~4 driven screws per slat with one quarter-turn lock. Nothing is welded; every slat stays individually removable, and a broken one swaps out in seconds instead of being unscrewed.

Concept sketch for discussion — not to scale, fastener style (cam / quarter-turn / spring detent) to be chosen with engineering. Time figures from recorded Seat Support step time (Sola, medium); ~16% assumes the step is fastening-dominated.
